About IDR

Where Academic Insight Meets Operational Reality

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute specialising in digital, cyber and technology risk roles across regulated and high-consequence sectors. We work at the intersection of academic excellence and applied practice — bridging the gap between theory and the complex risk environments practitioners face daily.

In partnership with a leading UK university, IDR brings together students, early-career professionals and seasoned practitioners to develop, test and deploy risk frameworks that are both academically grounded and operationally proven. Our model is built for organisations where risk cannot be hypothetical.
